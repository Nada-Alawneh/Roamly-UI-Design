PRIVACY & PERSONAL DATA

Your privacy, clearly explained.

Learn how Roamly handles information when you explore destinations, manage your account, and save trip ideas.

Effective 17 October 2026 Roamly privacy notice

How Roamly stores your information
Account, profile, settings, and itinerary details are stored in this browser. Roamly does not process payments or place supplier reservations, and contact or newsletter forms do not deliver messages to a Roamly service. Use a unique password and do not enter sensitive health, identity, or payment information.

01

Who this notice covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Roamly website and its travel-planning service. It explains what information is handled when you browse, manage profile details, and save trip ideas.

In this notice, “Roamly,” “we,” and “our” refer to the Roamly website and its travel-planning features.

02

Information Roamly handles

Depending on the features you choose, the browser may store:

Account and profile details: name, email address, phone number, and country or place of residence that you enter. Account records and password hashes are saved in browser storage; protect access to your device and use a unique password.

Trip and preference details: destinations, dates, traveler counts, budget, travel mood, saved itinerary content, and preferences used to display the planner and your account pages.

Technical and preference data: selected currency and cached exchange-rate data in local storage, temporary sign-in and planner state in session storage, and the position of the movable chat button for the current browser session.

Roamly does not request payment-card details. The Book Trip panel provides a cost estimate and does not submit reservations or process payments.

03

Why information is used

Roamly uses the information you enter to operate site features: create and display your account, show your profile, prepare or save itinerary ideas, remember settings, and apply the free-plan limit.

Currency selection may request current exchange-rate data so displayed amounts can be converted. The newsletter and contact forms are not connected to Roamly mailing or support systems. Do not use them to send confidential information; submitting a form may still send its fields to the website host as part of the browser request.

04

Browser storage and retention

Profile details, account records, preferences, and saved itineraries can remain in your browser’s local storage after you close the page. Temporary workflow information is kept in session storage and is generally cleared when that browser session ends. The data is not synced between your devices.

You can remove this locally stored information by signing out where available and clearing this site’s storage or browsing data in your browser. Clearing it may also remove saved itineraries and settings. Roamly does not maintain a central account database, so information deleted from your browser cannot be recovered.

When forms are submitted, the website host may handle ordinary web request data, such as the request itself and technical log information, under the host’s own retention practices. Roamly does not define or control the host’s log-retention period.

05

External services and disclosures

The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts and icon assets from jsDelivr. The currency tool requests exchange-rate data from the Open Exchange Rates API at open.er-api.com. When your browser contacts these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the requested resource, and handle it under their own privacy terms.

Your locally saved profile and itinerary data are not shared with these providers. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect the security and lawful operation of the service.

Because these providers operate their own infrastructure, a request may be processed outside Jordan. Their own policies and safeguards govern that processing.

06

Security

Browser storage is not encrypted by the Roamly application and can be accessed by someone who has access to your device or browser profile. Do not store sensitive information, use a unique password, and sign out on shared devices.

Sign-in uses locally stored account details and is not a secure identity service. We cannot promise that locally stored information will be immune to device compromise, browser extensions, or loss of the device.

07

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose what information to enter, browse without an account, and clear locally stored site data using your browser settings. You may also use the profile and settings pages to edit or remove some information saved in this browser.

Where applicable under Jordanian law, individuals may have rights that include being informed about and accessing their data, obtaining a copy, correcting or updating it, withdrawing prior consent, limiting processing, requesting erasure or concealment, and objecting to certain processing. These rights are subject to the law and its exceptions.

For a privacy question or request, email info@roamly.com. Most profile and trip information can only be viewed or deleted in the browser where it was entered. You may also consult the Jordan Personal Data Protection Directorate’s complaint and inquiry information.

08

Children’s information

Roamly is a general travel-planning service and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information into the site, please contact us so we can advise how to clear the browser data.

09

Changes and contact

We may revise this notice when the site or its data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Please review this page periodically for updates.

For privacy questions, contact info@roamly.com or visit our Contact Us page. Please do not include passwords, payment details, identity documents, or other sensitive information in a message.

10

Jordanian data-protection framework

This notice is written with reference to Jordan’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, which entered into force on 17 March 2024. Applicable rights and obligations depend on the circumstances and any mandatory rules that apply.

For the official law and related guidance, see the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship’s legislation and instructions and its Personal Data Protection FAQs.
