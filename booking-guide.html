GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Need help with something specific? Our contact page is the best place to start.

Contact Roamly support
Can I use the planner before I create an account?

Yes. Browse the site and customize the planner freely. Sign in is requested when you generate your first itinerary.

How many plans can I generate for free?

One itinerary generation is included per account. To generate more, compare the plans on our Pricing page.

Does Book Trip reserve flights or accommodation?

No. It opens your trip cost summary. No payment is processed and no supplier reservation is made through Roamly.

Where do I find a plan I saved?

Sign in, open the account menu, and choose My Itineraries to review your saved plans.
