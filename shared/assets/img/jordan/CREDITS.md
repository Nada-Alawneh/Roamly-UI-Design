# Jordan narrative media

## Destination chapter clips

Recorded footage was trimmed, cropped, and exported without audio. The Jerash, Dead Sea, Petra, and Aqaba excerpts were slowed for the chapter loops.

- Amman by Deror_avi, [Amman Citadel View](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amman_Citadel_View_P1090414.ogv), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- Jerash, the Dead Sea, Petra, and Aqaba by the Jordan Tourism Board, [Jordan The Bucket List](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jordan_The_Bucket_List_30s.webm), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Madaba by Deror_avi, [Byzantine floor mosaic map at St. George Church](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Byzantine_floor_mosaic_map_at_St._George_Church_Madaba_P1090118.ogv), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- Wadi Rum by Jorge Láscar, [Climbing a sand dune](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:VIDEO_-_Climbing_a_sand_duna_barefooted.webm), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

The As-Salt, Ajloun, Umm Qais, Karak, and Dana loops animate their original chapter photographs with a camera move. The photographer and license for each derivative are the same as its source below.
